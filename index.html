What services do you offer? +

I offer full-stack development services including web application development, UI/UX design implementation, database architecture, API development, and performance optimization. I specialize in modern technologies and best practices to deliver scalable, maintainable solutions.

Project timelines vary depending on scope and complexity. Small projects typically take 2-4 weeks, medium projects 1-3 months, and larger applications 3-6+ months. I'll provide a detailed estimate after discussing your specific requirements.

Do you work with existing codebases? +

Absolutely! I have extensive experience maintaining, refactoring, and improving existing projects. Whether you need bug fixes, feature additions, performance improvements, or a complete redesign, I can help modernize your codebase.

What languages do you specialize in? +

I specialize in modern web technologies including React, Node.js, JavaScript/TypeScript, HTML5, CSS3, and various databases. I'm also comfortable learning new languages quickly to meet your project needs and stay current with industry standards.

How do you handle project communication? +

Communication is key to project success. I provide regular updates, documentation, and I am available through your preferred channels (Discord, email, etc.). I ensure you're always informed about project progress and can address concerns promptly.
